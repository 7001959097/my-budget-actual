My Budget vs Actual v1.4 PWA

Offline-first monthly budget tracker with Cashbook.
- Existing Category, Item, Expense and Savings/Investment budgets and actuals are preserved.
- Fixed Expense budgets carry forward automatically to later months.
- Cashbook supports Advance Received, Loan Repayment, Money Lent, Daily Expense and other Credit/Debit. No Opening Balance or Salary Credit entry is required.
- Monthly closing balance automatically carries forward as the next month opening balance, starting from zero.
- Cashbook CSV exports all transactions with Credit, Debit and running balance.
- Backup/Restore includes existing budget data and Cashbook data.
- The spelling “Train monthly” is corrected automatically.
- Data is stored locally in the browser. Use Backup/Restore to transfer between devices.
